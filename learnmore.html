More about us

We were originally established by Mace Wakai in 1873 under the name Mace's Munitions Supplies as a small,
high-quality German manufacturer of artillery. Since then, we've shifted our focus to technology and electronics,
becoming a highly respected vendor of electronic parts, hardware, and even software. Not only that, but we have expanded into
other categories too, like pharma and food. We are also the holding company for
many subsidiaries, including Brhih Foods Inc., Ellipsis Staffing, Joey Corp, and of course, GammaTek Technology Corporation.
